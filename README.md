# flashback-server

A launcher and personal steam for all of your retro gaming needs.

## Have you ever wanted all your retro games, or ROMs in a single place?
I made this launcher specifically for that purpose, put your GOG games, or ROMs onto the server
then use the desktop client to make the experience as simple as using steam!
you can uninstall/reinstall the games, and have your controllers automatically detected as well as have 
your emulators configured by default! 

## The server:
- Automatically pulls metadata and reviews for your game.
- Holds all your roms.
- Gives you an easy to use web-interface to manage everything
## The launcher:
- Auto detects controllers and configures them in emualtors
- Allows you to manage you Library
- Manages and auto installs all your emulators in one place!
